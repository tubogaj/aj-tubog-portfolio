About

The short version of a longer story.

I started my career answering phones at a BPO in Taytay — not exactly where you'd expect a data analyst's story to begin. But years inside that operations floor taught me how much of a business's growth, or breakdown, quietly comes down to its processes and its data, and how well the two are connected.

That curiosity carried me from customer service into reporting and business intelligence, then into marketing database work for a Silicon Valley SaaS company, and eventually into marketing operations for a global HR platform based in Sydney. Along the way I've audited CRMs holding hundreds of thousands of records, untangled broken lead-routing logic, and turned messy spreadsheets into dashboards people actually trust.

Today I bring that same operator's eye to freelance and contract work — building dashboards, automations, internal tools, and websites for businesses that need to move faster than their current systems allow. I'm not chasing the newest framework; I'm chasing the fewest steps between a business having a problem and having a solution.
